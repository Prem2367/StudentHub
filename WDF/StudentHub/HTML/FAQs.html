FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

1. How can I apply for admission?

You can apply by visiting the Registration page and filling out the online registration form.

2. What courses are offered?

Our college offers BCA, BBA, B.Com, B.Tech, MCA, MBA, and several other undergraduate and postgraduate programs.

3. What are the college timings?

The college is open from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday to Saturday.

4. How can I check my attendance?

You can view your attendance by logging into the Student Dashboard.

5. Where can I find information about college events?

All upcoming events, workshops, and cultural activities are listed on the Events page.

6. Who should I contact for support?

You can contact the college administration office or email support@charusat.edu.in for assistance.
